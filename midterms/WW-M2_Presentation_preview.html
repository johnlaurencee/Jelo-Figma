Project Proposal Presentation

LalimSalita

A RAG-Based Contextual Vocabulary Tool for Difficult Filipino Words in Florante at Laura

Background of the Study

Vocabulary challenges in Florante at Laura

Grade 8 students encounter unfamiliar, deep, figurative, and context-dependent Filipino expressions while studying the literary work. The curriculum asks learners to understand vocabulary and interpret matatalinghagang pahayag, tayutay, and symbols.

Evidence of the Problem

Interest does not always equal understanding

Hermonio (2025)

Grade 8 students showed weaknesses in defining and interpreting Filipino words and statements.

Nimes (2024) and Andrade (2025)

Studies reported moderate understanding of Florante at Laura and showed that vocabulary interventions can improve learning.

Specific Problem Statement

Context changes meaning

Grade 8 students may struggle with unfamiliar, deep, and figurative Filipino words when their intended meanings depend on a particular line or stanza in Florante at Laura.

Research Question

Can LalimSalita generate accurate, contextual, simplified explanations?

Grounded Inputs

Validated dictionary definitions and relevant literary passages provide the evidence for each response.

Evaluation Criteria

Answer accuracy, contextual relevance, groundedness, explanation simplicity, retrieval relevance, and appropriate refusal.

Knowledge Base Sources

Reliable information before generation

KWF Diksiyonaryo ng Wikang Filipino

Provides validated Filipino word definitions.

Project Gutenberg: Florante at Laura

Provides literary passages and the context in which difficult words appear.

Test Plan

Questions used to evaluate the chatbot

  • Direct word-meaning questions
  • Context-based questions
  • Simplified-explanation questions
  • Out-of-domain questions

Proposed Model

Llama-SEA-LION-v3.5-8B-R

Language Support

Supports Filipino, English, Indonesian, Malay, Thai, Vietnamese, and other Southeast Asian languages.

Deployment

An 8-billion-parameter model with official GGUF quantized variants for more memory-efficient deployment.
