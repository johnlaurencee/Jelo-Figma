CHUNK JUSTIFICATION

Justification for the Selected Chunking Configuration
The LalimSalita Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) chatbot evaluated three chunking configurations to determine the most suitable chunk size and overlap for retrieving Filipino vocabulary definitions and contextual meanings. The tested configurations consisted of CFG1 (300-character chunk size and 50-character overlap), CFG2 (800-character chunk size and 150-character overlap), and CFG3 (500-character chunk size and 50-character overlap). Based on the updated retrieval evaluation involving eight in-scope vocabulary questions, CFG3 demonstrated the strongest overall performance, achieving an average Precision@3 of 41.67% and a Mean Reciprocal Rank (MRR) of 1.0000. CFG1 achieved a Precision@3 of 37.50% and an MRR of 1.0000, while CFG2 obtained a Precision@3 of 29.17% and an MRR of 0.7500. These results indicate that CFG3 retrieved the highest proportion of relevant chunks among the top three results while consistently ranking a relevant chunk first for all evaluated in-scope questions. The 500-character chunk size provided a practical balance between preserving relevant vocabulary information and limiting unrelated content within retrieved chunks. Compared with the smaller 300-character configuration, CFG3 was intended to reduce excessive fragmentation that could separate vocabulary terms from their definitions and contextual meanings. Meanwhile, compared with the larger 800-character configuration, CFG3 provided more focused retrieval units, which may have contributed to its higher Precision@3. Additionally, the 50-character overlap was intended to maintain continuity between adjacent chunks and reduce the possibility of losing important information at chunk boundaries. This is particularly relevant to LalimSalita, where vocabulary definitions and contextual interpretations must remain accessible to the retrieval system. Therefore, CFG3 was selected as the preferred chunking configuration for the LalimSalita RAG chatbot because it achieved the highest average Precision@3 while maintaining strong ranking effectiveness among the three tested configurations. The two out-of-scope questions were excluded from the average retrieval metric calculations because they were intended to evaluate the chatbot's ability to handle unsupported queries. Nevertheless, this selection is based on the evaluated configurations and a limited set of test questions. Further experiments using a larger question set and complete relevance judgments are recommended to validate standard Recall@3 and determine whether the selected chunk size and overlap remain optimal under broader testing conditions.
